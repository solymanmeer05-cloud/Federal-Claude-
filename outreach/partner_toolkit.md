## Leaflet text (A5, two sides)

**Side 1**

Go further, without giving up work.

[Provider] runs higher education courses for adults aged 21 and over, taught two days a week. Many courses need no formal qualifications: you'll have an informal interview and a short assessment instead.

**Side 2**

**How is it paid for?**
Eligible students can get a tuition fee loan through student finance, so there's nothing to pay upfront. You repay only once you earn above the repayment threshold, and payments stop if your income drops below it. You may also be able to get a maintenance loan for living costs. Eligibility depends on where you live and whether you've studied before. See gov.uk/student-finance.

## Staff briefing note (for partner staff)

- Who it's for: adults 21+, especially people in work who want to progress.
- What to say: "There's a part-time route into higher education you might be interested in; here's their number."
- What not to say: that it's free, that everyone gets in, or anything about benefits. Benefits questions go to the work coach or Citizens Advice.
- Never pass on anyone's details without their own signed consent on our form.
- We don't pay referral fees, and partners shouldn't accept any from anyone.
- Questions: [named contact, phone, email].
