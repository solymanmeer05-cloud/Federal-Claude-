# Texts, Calls and Enquiry Forms

## Text messages (SMS / WhatsApp)

**Only text someone who contacted you first and agreed to be texted** (for example, they ticked the box on the enquiry form below). Under UK PECR, marketing texts to people without prior consent are unlawful. Never text numbers from social media, lists or partners.

**After an enquiry (same day)**
Hi [first name], it's [your name] from [Provider]. Thanks for asking about our courses for adults. When's a good time for a quick call? Reply STOP to opt out.

**Reminder of a booked call**
Hi [first name], just a reminder that [your name] from [Provider] will call you [day] at [time] about courses. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Info session reminder**
Hi [first name], the information session you booked is at [venue], [date], [time]. See you there. Reply STOP to opt out.

**No answer after an enquiry (send once only)**
Hi [first name], [your name] from [Provider] here. I tried to call about the courses you asked about. Call me on [number] whenever suits. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Phone script: calling a partner organisation

Call the organisation's main or published number and ask for the person or team named in the email.

> "Hi, my name's [name], I'm the Community Partnerships Officer at [Provider]. I emailed [name] last week about a part-time route into higher education for the adults you support. Is now a good time for two minutes?"

If yes:
1. "Do you find the people you work with ever ask about getting qualifications while they keep working?"
2. Listen. Then: "Our courses are for adults 21+, taught two days a week, and many don't need previous qualifications. Tuition can be paid through student finance for eligible students."
3. "The easiest way to help is sharing a poster or hosting a short info session. Anyone interested contacts us directly; we don't need any client details and we don't pay referral fees."
4. "Would a 15-minute call with your team, or some posters, be more useful?"

If no or not now: "No problem at all, thanks for your time." Note it and don't call again unless invited.

**Voicemail**
> "Hi [name], it's [name] from [Provider], about a part-time route into higher education for adults you support. No rush; my number is [number]. Thanks."

## Phone script: calling someone who enquired

> "Hi [first name], it's [name] from [Provider]. You asked about our courses for adults; is now still a good time?"

- Ask what they're hoping to do and how many days they can study.
- Explain courses, the two-day timetable and the entry interview.
- Explain student finance accurately: a loan, nothing upfront, repaid only above the threshold, eligibility depends on residency and previous study. Point to gov.uk/student-finance.
- Never pressure, never say places or funding are running out, never ask for bank details or money.
- Benefits questions: "Please check with your work coach or Citizens Advice before applying."
- Next step: book the entry interview, or send information and agree when to follow up.

## Enquiry form consent wording

Use on your website, partner QR-code page and paper forms at info sessions.

> [Provider] will use your details to respond to your enquiry about our courses. We'll keep them for [period] and won't share them with anyone else. See our privacy notice: [link].
>
> ☐ I'm happy for [Provider] to contact me by **phone**
> ☐ I'm happy for [Provider] to contact me by **text message**
> ☐ I'm happy for [Provider] to contact me by **email**
>
> You can stop contact at any time by replying STOP or emailing [address].

Leave every box unticked by default.
