# Partner Emails

Cold email sequences for organisations across the UK. Harvey writes its own personalised versions using `skills/uk_adult_learner_courses.md`; these are the hand-written reference versions to approve against, or to send yourself.

Replace everything in [square brackets]. Each email is plain text, under 75 words, with one question. Harvey adds the opt-out line and your postal address underneath.

Sending rules: write to a named person's work address at an organisation (never a personal Gmail or Hotmail). Three emails at most per contact, then stop. Honour "no thanks" immediately.

---
