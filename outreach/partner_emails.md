# Partner Emails

Cold email sequences from Solyman at Platinum Education to organisations across the UK. Harvey writes its own personalised versions using `skills/uk_adult_learner_courses.md`; these are the hand-written reference versions to approve against, or to send yourself.

Replace everything in [square brackets]. [provider] means a course provider Platinum Education has a written agreement with. Each email is plain text, under 75 words, with one question. Harvey adds the opt-out line and your company details underneath.

Sending rules: write to a named person's work address at an organisation (never a personal Gmail or Hotmail). Three emails at most per contact, then stop. Honour "no thanks" immediately.

Signature on every email:

```
Solyman
Student Consultant, Platinum Education
Independent education consultancy · [phone] · [website]
```
