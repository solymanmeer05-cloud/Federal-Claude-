# Reply Templates

Short answers to common replies. Harvey drafts its own from these; every reply waits in the Outbox for your approval.

**"Yes, tell me more"**
Thanks [First name]. Would [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] suit for a 15-minute call? I'll explain how we help people and what courses there are.

**"Can you just send information?"**
Of course. I've attached a short summary and a poster. Anyone interested can contact me directly on [phone] or [email].

**"Who are you exactly?"**
Platinum Education is an education consultancy. We help adults who didn't get the GCSEs or A-levels they wanted get onto university courses. There are over 400 courses across the UK with multiple awarding bodies.

**"How are you paid?" / "Do students pay you?"** (only when asked)
We're paid by the universities we're partnered with. Students never pay us anything.

**"Is it free?"**
Tuition can be paid through student finance for eligible students, so there's nothing to pay upfront. It's a loan, repaid only once someone earns above the threshold. gov.uk/student-finance has the details.

**"Do you pay for referrals?"**
No. Partners just share information, and anyone interested contacts us directly.

**"Will it affect benefits?"**
That depends on each person's situation, so it's best to check with their work coach or Citizens Advice.

**"Not now"**
No problem, thanks for letting me know. Shall I check back in [month]?

**"No thanks" / "Unsubscribe"**
Harvey stops automatically and never emails them again.

**Complaints, safeguarding or legal issues:** don't reply. Harvey passes these to you.
