# Reply Templates

How to answer common replies from partner organisations. Harvey's reply handler drafts its own answers from these principles, and every answer waits in the Outbox for approval.

---

**"Yes, tell me more" / "Happy to chat"**

Thanks [First name]. Would [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] work for a 15-minute call? I'll cover the courses, how entry interviews work and how student finance works, then we can see what suits [Organisation].

**"Can you just send information?"**

Of course. The attached one-pager covers the courses, who they're for and how people apply. If any of your clients are interested, they can contact us directly at [phone / email / web page]. Would a few printed posters help as well?

**"Is this legitimate? We've seen dodgy outfits recruiting for these courses."**

Fair question, and I'm glad you asked. I work for [Provider] directly; we're not an agent. Our courses are [validated by / delivered with] [University]. Students apply through the official student finance service, and we never ask anyone for money or bank details. Happy to send our registration details or speak on a call so you can check us out.

**"Do you pay for referrals?"**

No. We don't pay referral fees, and we don't offer students incentives to enrol. Partners just share information, and anyone interested speaks to us directly.

**"Can we send you a list of interested clients?"**

Thanks, but please don't send anyone's details on their behalf. It's simpler and safer for people to contact us themselves at [contact]. If someone wants you to pass on their details, they can fill in our consent form ([link]) and you can send that.

**"Our clients are on benefits. Will studying affect them?"**

That depends on each person's situation, so the best people to ask are their work coach or Citizens Advice before they apply. We can explain how the course and student finance work, but we can't give benefits advice.

**"Is it free?"**

Tuition is paid through student finance for eligible students, so there's nothing to pay upfront. It's a loan, repaid only once someone earns above the repayment threshold, and repayments stop if their income drops below it. Eligibility depends on residency and previous study; gov.uk/student-finance has the details.

**"Not now / maybe later"**

Thanks for letting me know. Shall I check back in [month], or would you rather I didn't?

**"No thanks" / "Unsubscribe" / "Remove me"**

Harvey stops automatically. If you answer by hand: "Understood, I've removed you and won't contact you again." Then stop.

**Anything about complaints, safeguarding, a vulnerable person, legal action or the press**

Don't reply. Harvey hands these to a human; take them to your manager or compliance lead.
